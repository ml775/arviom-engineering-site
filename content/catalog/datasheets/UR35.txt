Industrial Router

UR35




                    UR35 is a cost-effective industrial cellular router with embedded
                    intelligent features that are designed for multifarious M2M/IoT
                    applications. Global WCDMA and 4G LTE carrier supported make
                    this drop-in connectivity a great help for operators in maximizing
                    uptime.

                    Integrating embedded cellular modem and dual SIM function, the
                    UR35 provides 3G/4G cellular network with maximum 150 Mbps
                    download and 50 Mbps uplink, it also has 5 fast Ethernet ports and
                    supports Wi-Fi that compliance with 802.11b/g/n standard. All
                    these capabilities deliver users an uninterrupted internet access.

                    Easy deployment and comprehensive remote device management
                    makes UR35 versatile in most of IoT/M2M applications.
